Everything you need to know about Viking Tub.

Ask Anything
How long does setup take?
Full assembly takes under 3 minutes. Simply unfold the tub, inflate the top ring using the included pump, attach the drain tap, and fill with water. No tools needed.
Do I need a chiller or can I use ice?
You can use ice or a chiller — Viking Tub is compatible with both. Most customers use ice bags, which are very effective thanks to the thermo lid keeping temperatures low for hours.
Can I use it indoors and outdoors?
Absolutely. Viking Tub is designed to work perfectly indoors and outdoors. The all-weather cover protects it outside from debris and weather elements.
How do I clean it?
The UV-resistant interior lining is very easy to clean with standard cleaning products. Drain the water using the quick-release tap, wipe down the interior, and allow it to dry before storing.
Where do you ship and how long does it take?
We offer free shipping throughout Colombia. Orders are processed and dispatched within 3–5 business days. You will receive a tracking number once your order ships.
Can I return it if I am not satisfied?
Yes. We offer a 30-day return policy. If you are not satisfied with your purchase for any reason, contact us within 30 days and we will arrange a full refund.
How cold should the water be?
For beginners, 15–18°C (59–64°F) is ideal. Advanced users typically use 10–15°C (50–59°F). The Wim Hof method recommends starting gradually and building up your cold tolerance over time.
